### S1 — Battle Of Britain (Enemy Losses)
Author / issuing body: UK Parliament, House of Commons (Hansard); statement by Secretary of State for Air, Philip Noel-Baker  
Date: 14 May 1947  
Source type: Primary (parliamentary record quoting German High Command figures)  
Exact URL retrieved: https://hansard.parliament.uk/%E2%80%8CCommons/1947-05-14/debates/9be4514a-e88e-49d8-b460-d7722f598530/BattleOfBritain%28EnemyLosses%29  
Confidence: HIGH  
What it establishes:  
- Documented fact: Official postwar statement to Parliament that German records show, for 10 July–31 October 1940 (the Battle of Britain): 2,376 aircraft “put out of action,” of which 1,733 were destroyed and 643 damaged. It contrasts this with contemporary RAF claims of 2,692 destroyed; and provides a breakdown by sub-period and “biggest days,” listing RAF estimates vs German recorded destroyed and damaged.  
Important evidence / qualification:  
- The tabulated breakdown shows, for example, 15 Aug (RAF estimate 183; German losses 76 destroyed/9 damaged), 18 Aug (155; 71/23), 31 Aug (94; 39/14), 15 Sep (185; 56/21), and 1–31 Oct totals (RAF estimate 260; German 325 destroyed/163 damaged). It explicitly notes bracketed figures were those admitted in German communiqués at the time. The source is an official ministerial statement citing German High Command records.

## 3. SOURCE DISAGREEMENTS AND EVIDENTIAL CONFLICTS

Issue: RAF fighter losses in the initial Channel phase (preliminary phase)  
- Account A (S3 — Official history appendix): Totals for 10 July–12 August show 150 British fighter losses.  
- Account B (S7 — RAF Museum timeline): States that during the Kanalkampf the Luftwaffe lost 286 aircraft as against RAF Fighter Command losses of 148 aircraft.  
Assessment: The figures are very close but not identical. Both refer to the same broad period; S3 provides a tabulated official history total, while S7 presents an institutional summary. The 2‑aircraft discrepancy remains unresolved here.

Issue: 18 August 1940 German losses categorisation  
- Account A (S2 — RAF Museum “Hardest Day”): “69 German aircraft were destroyed or damaged beyond repair.”  
- Account B (S1 — Hansard 14 May 1947): For 18 August, German losses recorded as 71 destroyed and 23 damaged (total 94 “put out of action,” but “damaged” is not identical to “damaged beyond repair”).  
Assessment: Different categories/definitions are being used. S1 distinguishes “destroyed” vs “damaged”; S2 aggregates “destroyed or damaged beyond repair.” Not directly comparable; do not conflate.

Issue: Availability of RAF October 1940 fighter aircraft loss totals in the retrieved official appendices  
- Account A (S6 — Official history Appendix XXIV): Provides daily RAF fighter losses for 7–30 September; lists October as “minor raids” without daily/total figures.  
- Account B: No retrieved official-history table within this session provides RAF October fighter loss totals.  
Assessment: RAF October loss totals remain UNVERIFIED here; only German October totals (325 destroyed/163 damaged) are securely established from S1.

If further conflicts are identified in later research, record them separately.
